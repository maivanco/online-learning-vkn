# 03. Student's Learning Handbook

This handbook guides Dhamma practitioners through the 5-step learning path to help you study, practice, and graduate successfully.

---

## 1. Login & Accessing Your Classes

1. Visit the portal homepage and click **Sign In**.
2. Enter your **Username** or **Email** and password provided by the monastery.
3. Upon login, you will arrive at your **Student Dashboard**.
4. View your enrolled classes along with your percentage (%) progress bar.
5. Click on any class to start learning.

---

## 2. The 5-Step Sequential Learning Path

To foster steady wisdom, the portal guides you through 5 sequential steps:

```
[Step 1: Reading] ──> [Step 2: Video] ──> [Step 3: Practice] ──> [Step 4: Final Exam] ──> [Step 5: Mistake Mastery]
```

### Step 1: Self-Study Reading Material (Reading)
- Open a lesson and click the **"Reading"** tab.
- Read canonical texts, commentaries, and key takeaways carefully.
- Once finished, click the amber button: **"Complete Reading"**.
- *Result*: Unlocks **Step 2: Video Lecture** for this lesson.

### Step 2: Watch the Video Lecture (Video)
- Switch to the **"Video"** tab.
- Listen attentively to the teacher's discourse. Taking notes is encouraged.
- When finished, click **"Complete Video"**.
- *Result*: Unlocks **Step 3: Practice Quizzes** for this lesson.

### Step 3: Practice Quizzes (Practice)
- Switch to the **"Practice"** tab.
- Read each question and select the answer you believe is correct.
- Click **"Check Answer"**:
  - If correct: A green indicator appears.
  - If incorrect: A red indicator appears along with an in-depth **Explanation**.
- Complete all practice questions in the lesson.
- *Result*: The lesson gets a **green checkmark** on the curriculum list!

### Step 4: Class Final Examination (Final Exam)
- **Unlocking Condition**: You must complete all 3 steps (Reading, Video, Practice) across **ALL lessons** in the class.
- When ready, the **"Take Final Exam"** button turns amber and active on your dashboard.
- Click to begin:
  - Keep an eye on the **countdown timer** at the top of your screen!
  - Answer multiple-choice questions and write reflections for essay questions.
  - Click **"Submit Exam"** before the timer runs out.
- *Result*: Multiple choice is graded immediately; essay questions are sent to your teacher for review.

### Step 5: Master Incorrect Questions (Mistake Mastery)
- Any question you miss during quizzes or exams is automatically saved to your personal **Mistake Review Bank**.
- Visit this area anytime to review the teacher's explanations and re-attempt the questions until you achieve 100% mastery.
- *Result*: Cultivates unshakable right view and clears all doubts.

---

## 3. Asking Questions to Your Teacher

- If you encounter a difficult concept, use the **"Ask Instructor"** form at the bottom of the lesson.
- When your teacher replies, the answer will appear directly in your lesson view.

---

## 4. Graduation & Certificates

- Once your teacher finishes grading your essay questions, your final combined score will display on your dashboard.
- Your enrollment status will officially change to **"Graduated / Completed"**.
