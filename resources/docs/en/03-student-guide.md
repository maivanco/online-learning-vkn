# 03. Student's Learning Handbook

This handbook guides Dhamma practitioners through the 5-step learning path, Buddhist Dharma Name registration, taking timed final examinations with auto-submit protection, and accessing verified Buddhist graduation certificates.

---

## 1. Registration & Buddhist Practitioner Profile

### Account Registration
1. Visit the portal homepage and click **"Register"** in the top navigation bar.
2. Complete your registration details:
   - **Full Legal Name**: Your name as recorded on official identification.
   - **Buddhist Dharma Name (`dhamma_name`)**: Your Buddhist refuge name (e.g., *Tam An, Dieu Thien, Nhuan Phap*). This spiritual name will be prominently engraved on your graduation certificate. You may leave this blank if you have not formally taken refuge.
   - **Username** & **Email Address**: Used for portal authentication.
   - **Phone Number, Gender, Date of Birth & Physical Address**: Enables the monastery to maintain records and send physical study gifts or paper certificates when applicable.
   - **Password**: Minimum 8 characters.
3. Alternatively, you may choose one-click login via **Google OAuth**.

---

## 2. Student Dashboard

Upon logging in, you are greeted by your **Student Dashboard**:
- Displays all enrolled cohorts and subjects.
- A visual progress bar depicting your percentage (%) of completion.
- Assigned Instructor details and cohort status.
- **"Continue Learning"** button: Resumes current lessons in progress.
- **"View Certificate"** button: Appears automatically once you have completed all lessons and passed the final exam with a qualifying grade.

---

## 3. The 5-Step Sequential Learning Path

To foster steady comprehension, the portal enforces a 5-step sequential learning pipeline:

```
[Step 1: Reading] ──> [Step 2: Video] ──> [Step 3: Practice] ──> [Step 4: Final Exam] ──> [Step 5: Mistake Mastery]
```

### Step 1: Self-Study Reading Material (Reading)
- Open a lesson and click the **"Reading"** tab.
- Read canonical texts, Pāḷi doctrinal definitions, and commentary notes crafted by your teacher.
- When you have digested the text, click the amber button: **"Complete Reading"**.
- *Result*: The system records completion and unlocks **Step 2: Video Lecture** for this lesson.

### Step 2: Watch the Video Lecture (Video)
- Switch to the **"Video"** tab.
- Listen attentively to the Venerable Monk or Nun's discourse. Taking notes is encouraged.
- When finished, click **"Complete Video"**.
- *Result*: The system unlocks **Step 3: Practice Quizzes** for this lesson.

### Step 3: Practice Quizzes (Practice)
- Switch to the **"Practice"** tab.
- Read each quiz question and select the answer you believe is correct.
- Click **"Check Answer"**:
  - If correct: A celebratory green indicator appears.
  - If incorrect: A red indicator appears alongside an in-depth **Explanation** so you understand the doctrinal rationale immediately.
- Complete all practice questions in the lesson.
- *Result*: The lesson is marked with a **green completed checkmark**!

### Step 4: Class Final Examination (Final Exam)
- **Unlocking Prerequisite**: You must achieve 100% completion across all lessons (Reading, Video, and Practice completed in every lesson).
- When eligible, the **"Take Final Exam"** button illuminates amber on your dashboard:
  - **Timed Examination**: The screen displays a live countdown timer (e.g., 45 minutes) synchronized with the server.
  - **Multiple-Choice Questions**: Select your chosen answers carefully.
  - **Essay Questions** (if included): Write your philosophical reflections and doctrinal understanding.
  - **Autosave & Auto-Submit Protection**: If the countdown clock reaches `00:00:00` before you click submit, the system **automatically posts all your currently selected answers to the server**, safeguarding your exam from accidental expiration.
  - Click **"Submit Exam"** when you are finished.
- *Result*: Multiple-choice items are scored instantaneously; essay items are forwarded to your instructor for thoughtful spiritual grading and feedback.

### Step 5: Master Incorrect Questions (Mistake Mastery)
- Any question missed during lesson quizzes or final exams is recorded in your personal **Mistake Review Bank**.
- Revisit this section anytime to review doctrinal explanations and re-attempt missed questions until you achieve 100% mastery.
- *Spiritual Benefit*: Cultivates unwavering Right View (*Sammā-diṭṭhi*) and clears lingering doctrinal misunderstandings.

## 4. Asking Questions to Your Instructor

- If you encounter complex doctrinal concepts while studying, use the **"Ask Instructor"** form located at the foot of each lesson.
- Enter your question and click **Submit**.
- When your teacher replies, their answer appears directly inside the lesson view.

---

## 5. Viewing, Printing & Verifying Buddhist Certificates

Once your instructor grades your essay questions and your final grade reaches at least 50% (5.0/10), you officially graduate!

### Accessing Your Certificate
- On your Student Dashboard or on the Exam Completion screen, click **"View Certificate"**.
- The portal renders an official, ornate Buddhist Certificate of Completion from Vien Khong Ni Monastery featuring:
  - **Student Legal Name** and **Buddhist Dharma Name** (`dhamma_name`).
  - **Course Title** and **Class Cohort**.
  - **Date of Completion**.
  - **Final Grade** and **Distinction Honour**:
    - **High Distinction / Xuất sắc**: Final Grade $\ge 9.0$
    - **Distinction / Giỏi**: Final Grade $8.0 \le \text{Grade} < 9.0$
    - **Credit / Khá**: Final Grade $7.0 \le \text{Grade} < 8.0$
    - **Pass / Đạt**: Final Grade $5.0 \le \text{Grade} < 7.0$
  - **Unique Certificate Code**: (e.g., `VKN-CERT-A1B2C3`).
  - **Live Verification QR Code**: Anyone can scan this QR code with their mobile camera to open the official public verification page on the monastery website, guaranteeing the certificate's authenticity.

### Printing & PDF Export
- Click **"Print / Save PDF"** at the top right of the certificate.
- The browser opens a print preview perfectly formatted for A4 landscape orientation, allowing you to print for framing or save as a digital PDF archive.
