# 01. System Overview & Core Philosophy

Welcome Venerable Monks, Nuns, Instructors, and Dhamma Practitioners to the **Buddhist Online Learning Portal - Vien Khong Ni Monastery**.

This documentation is written in a simple, friendly style for non-technical users, explaining how the platform operates and how teachers and students interact across all modules.

---

## 1. Platform Purpose & Vision

The platform bridges timeless Dhamma wisdom with modern digital education tools:
- **Standardized Learning Roadmap**: Students study sequentially, from reading scriptures and watching video lectures to practicing quizzes and taking final exams.
- **Efficiency for Teachers**: Automated grading for multiple-choice questions, real-time student progress tracking, and streamlined essay grading.
- **Dedicated Spiritual Learning Environment**: Practitioners worldwide can self-study and practice anytime, anywhere on computers or mobile phones.

---

## 2. Educational Philosophy: Hearing – Contemplation – Practice

The platform embodies the classical Buddhist threefold wisdom:

1. **Suta-maya panna (Wisdom through Reading & Hearing)**:
   - Self-study reading canonical texts and commentaries (Reading tab).
   - Listening to comprehensive Dharma discourses by teachers (Video Lecture tab).
2. **Cinta-maya panna (Wisdom through Reflection & Inquiry)**:
   - Reinforcing understanding through immediate-feedback practice quizzes.
   - Sending reflections and questions to teachers via the lesson feedback form.
3. **Bhavana-maya panna (Wisdom through Realization & Mastery)**:
   - Taking the comprehensive class final exam (multiple-choice and essays).
   - Reviewing and mastering questions answered incorrectly until full comprehension is achieved.

---

## 3. The Three User Roles

| Role | Title | Core Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Administrator** | System Admin | Manages overall system configuration, user accounts, role permissions, course categories, and media library assets. |
| **Teacher** | Instructor / Giao Tho | Manages assigned classes, enrolls students, creates lessons with rich text and videos, contributes questions, monitors progress, and grades essay exams. |
| **Student** | Learner / Practitioner | Follows the 5-step sequential roadmap: Reading $\rightarrow$ Video $\rightarrow$ Practice Quizzes $\rightarrow$ Final Exam $\rightarrow$ Mistake Mastery. |

---

## 4. Key Concepts to Know

- **Course**: A complete Buddhist subject (e.g. *Majjhima Nikaya*, *Satipatthana Meditation*, *Basic Dhamma Fundamentals*). A course contains sequential lessons.
- **Lesson**: A bite-sized teaching unit within a course, comprising Reading Content, a Video Lecture, and Practice Quizzes.
- **Class**: A live intake or cohort linked to a Course (e.g. *Meditation 101 - Cohort 2026*). A class has an assigned instructor and enrolled students.
- **Progress Matrix**: A live dashboard table allowing teachers to instantly see which students have finished reading, watched videos, or passed quizzes.
