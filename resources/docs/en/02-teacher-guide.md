# 02. Teacher's Operational Manual

This manual provides step-by-step instructions for Instructors and Administrators on managing courses, classes, student progression, and examinations.

---

## 1. Creating Courses & Adding Lessons

### Step 1: Open Courses & Lessons
- In the left sidebar, click **"Courses & Lessons"**.
- Click **"Add Course"** to create a new subject.
- Enter Course Title, Category (e.g. *Sutta, Meditation, Doctrine*), a summary description, and upload a cover thumbnail.

### Step 2: Add Sequential Lessons
- Click on your newly created course to view its lesson manager.
- Click **"Add Lesson"**:
  - **Lesson Title**: e.g., *Lesson 1: The Threefold Refuge and Precepts*.
  - **Reading Material**: Use the rich text editor to format scripture passages (bold, italics, blockquotes for verses, bullet points).
  - **Video Link**: Paste the YouTube video link of the Dharma discourse.
  - **Order**: Enter a sequence number so lessons appear in logical order.
- Click **Save Lesson**.

> **Tip**: Quote canonical verses in blockquote containers with clear sub-headings so learners can read comfortably on smartphones.

---

## 2. Managing Classes & Enrolling Students

### Step 1: Create a New Class
- Navigate to **"Classes & Progress"** (Main Admin Dashboard).
- Click **"Create New Class"**:
  - Select the underlying Course.
  - Name the class (e.g. *Dhamma Fundamentals - Intake 1 / 2026*).
  - Select the assigned Teacher.
- Click **Create Class**.

### Step 2: Enroll Students
- On the class card, click **"Manage"**.
- In the **"Add Student"** section, select registered student accounts and click **Add**. The class will instantly appear on the student's dashboard.

### Step 3: Class Lock Mechanism
- At the top of the Class Detail page, toggle the **"Lock Class"** button.
- **When Unlocked**: Students can study, submit steps, and take exams.
- **When Locked**: Students can review past lessons, but cannot submit new progress or exam attempts. Ideal when a term ends or during grade evaluation.

---

## 3. Building the Question Bank

1. Click **"Question Bank"** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"Create Question"**:
   - Associate the question with a Course and an optional Lesson.
   - Choose Question Type: **Multiple Choice** or **Essay**.
3. **For Multiple Choice**:
   - Enter the question prompt.
   - Fill in 4 options (A, B, C, D) and mark the correct answer.
   - Fill in **Detailed Explanation**: This is shown when learners review missed questions to help them understand the reasoning.
4. **For Essay Questions**:
   - Provide the prompt instructing students to reflect or articulate their understanding.

---

## 4. Monitoring Progress & Grading Final Exams

### Progress Matrix
On the Class Detail page, inspect the student table:
- **Reading**: Green checkmark when the student confirms reading completion.
- **Video**: Green checkmark when the video lecture is watched.
- **Practice**: Displays the practice quiz score for each lesson.
- **Status**: Updates to **"Ready for Exam"** once all lessons are completed.

### Grading Essay Exams
When a student completes their final exam:
1. Click **"View Exam Result"** on the student's row.
2. Multiple-choice questions are already scored automatically.
3. For **Essay questions**: Read the written reflection, input a score (0 to 10), and write constructive spiritual feedback.
4. Click **"Save Grade"**: The system combines the scores into a **Final Grade** and marks the student as **"Graduated / Completed"**.

---

## 5. Responding to Student Inquiries

- When students have questions while studying, they submit notes via the lesson feedback form.
- Go to **"Courses & Lessons"** $\rightarrow$ switch to the **"Student Feedback"** tab.
- Read the question and submit a compassionate response. The reply appears directly in the student's lesson player.
