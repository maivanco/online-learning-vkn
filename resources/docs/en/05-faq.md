# 05. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Common questions encountered by Instructors and Students along with practical answers.

---

### Question 1: I am a student. Why is the "Take Final Exam" button disabled?

**Answer**:
The final exam unlocks only after you achieve **100% completion across all lessons** in that class.
- Double-check every lesson to ensure you clicked **"Complete Reading"**, **"Complete Video"**, and answered the practice quizzes.
- All lessons must show a green completed checkmark.
- Also, confirm with your teacher that the class has not been set to "Locked".

---

### Question 2: Where does an instructor grade student essay answers?

**Answer**:
1. Go to **"Classes & Progress"** in the admin sidebar.
2. Click **"Manage"** on the relevant class.
3. In the student table, locate the student and click **"View Exam Result"**.
4. Scroll to the Essay Questions section, read the student's submission, input a score (0 to 10), and write your feedback.
5. Click **"Save Grade"** to calculate the final grade and record graduation.

---

### Question 3: Can students retake lesson practice quizzes multiple times?

**Answer**:
**Yes**. Practice quizzes are designed for reflective comprehension. Students can retake them freely without penalty to ensure they thoroughly understand the teachings.

---

### Question 4: What if a student loses internet connection during an exam?

**Answer**:
- Answers selected in the browser are cached temporarily. As soon as connectivity returns, students can resume.
- However, the overall countdown timer continues to run on server time, so students should reconnect promptly to submit on time.

---

### Question 5: How does an administrator reset a student's forgotten password?

**Answer**:
1. Navigate to **"Users & Roles"**.
2. Find the user by name or username.
3. Click **"Change Password"**, type the new password, and click Save.
4. The student can now sign in immediately using their username and the new password.

---

### Question 6: Does the portal work on smartphones and tablets?

**Answer**:
**Yes, completely**. The entire application is mobile-responsive and functions smoothly on smartphones (iOS/Android), tablets, and desktop computers alike.
